Every cell is a real hull-plate thickness in millimetres. Green plates you can punch through; red plates bounce you. Read the colour, aim for green — and watch forOutliertanks whose armor breaks the class pattern.

≤ 40 mm— most guns & AT pen it
41–80 mm— needs a real gun / APCR
> 80 mm— bounces most shots

1

Once a shellpenetrates, damage is flat RNG anywhere inside. The whole game is getting the pen — thickness (and slope) is what stops you.

2

Shoot thegun/mantlet first: roughly an 80% chance the enemy can't fire, ~20% chance of a longer reload.

3

If it runs, kill thetracks or engine, then flank at leisure.HEbarely scratches armor — loadAPCRfor penetration.

4

Numbers are flat plate thickness.Sloped-tagged tanks resist more than the number suggests — flank rather than trade head-on.
