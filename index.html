Where to
Shoot It

Read the colored strip on each tank: green is where your shell goes through. Front plates are thickest, sides thinner, rear and roof thinnest — put the round where the metal isn't.

Shoot here — reliable penetration
Thick — needs APCR or a good angle
Won't pen — don't waste shells
1

Once a shell penetrates, damage is flat RNG anywhere. The whole game is getting the pen, not where you land it.

2

Load APCR (red-stripe) rounds for the extra penetration. Angled armor barely helps here, so wiggling won't save them.

3

Every hull also has soft tracks, turret ring, and cannon. Disable the gun first, then work the body.

4

Flank whenever possible. A tank showing you its side or rear is dead no matter how thick its front is.

Penetrability reflects the community's post–Armor 2.0 testing. Front / Side / Rear / Roof are the four hull facings; the strip shows how each faces up. Individual builds vary — cross-check the in-game armor viewer.
