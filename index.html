Shoot It

Every cell below is a real hull-plate thickness in millimetres, pulled from the game's vehicle stats. Green plates you can punch through; red plates bounce you. Read the colour, aim for green.

≤ 40 mm — most guns & AT pen it
41–80 mm — needs a real gun / APCR
> 80 mm — bounces most shots
1

Once a shell penetrates, damage is flat RNG anywhere inside. The whole game is getting the pen — thickness (and slope) is what stops you.

2

Shoot the gun/mantlet first: the wiki tanker guide puts it at ~80% chance the enemy can't fire, ~20% chance of a longer reload.

3

If it runs, kill the tracks or engine, then flank at leisure. HE barely scratches armor — load APCR for penetration.

4

Numbers are flat plate thickness. Sloped fronts (Tiger II, T-70, T-34) resist more than the number suggests — flank rather than trade head-on.
